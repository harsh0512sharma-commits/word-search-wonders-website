Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Word Search Wonders offline?
Yes! Word Search Wonders is an offline-first puzzle game. All themes, chapters, word grids, and levels are stored locally on your device. An internet connection is only needed when loading ads for bonus coins or purchasing coin packs through Google Play.
How do I restore my purchases on a new device?
Open the game on your new device (signed into the same Google Play account), navigate to Settings or Shop, and tap Restore Purchases. Your "Remove Ads" unlock will automatically sync.
How do I delete or reset all my game data?
Go to Settings > Data Management > Delete / Reset Saved Game Data. Confirm the prompt, and all your puzzle progress, coins, and local settings will be wiped clean.
Where is my data stored?
Your gameplay progress is stored exclusively in your device's internal storage sandbox. We do not store your game data or personal information on external servers.
