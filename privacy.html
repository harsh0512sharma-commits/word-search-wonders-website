Word Search Wonders

Privacy Policy • Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Word Search Wonders ("we," "our," or "the App"). We believe in transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy describes what data is stored locally on your device, what data is processed by third-party developer services, and your rights regarding that data.

Key Summary: Gameplay progress, levels, and coins are saved locally on your device. The App does not have a user account or login system. Network connectivity is used exclusively for third-party services: displaying advertisements through Google AdMob and processing optional purchases through Google Play.

1. Local Game Data Stored on Your Device

Word Search Wonders stores your gameplay state locally in your device's private application sandbox:

  • Puzzle Progression: Completed level IDs, unlocked chapters and themes, star ratings, and words found.
  • Economy & Rewards: In-game coin balance, mystery gift countdowns, and available hint counts.
  • Settings & Preferences: Audio volume toggles, haptic feedback toggle, dark theme toggle, and terms acknowledgement status.
  • Entitlement Records: A local flag indicating whether the non-consumable "Remove Ads" product is active on this device.

This local game data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server operated by Word Search Wonders.

2. Third-Party Services & Network Data Collection

The App integrates two third-party developer libraries provided by Google LLC:

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded video ads to support the game. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information:

  • Device Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) and App Set ID for ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: Derived by Google from your IP address to deliver regionally relevant ads and satisfy local advertising compliance laws. The App itself does not request or access GPS or precise location permissions.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad load latency, crash logs associated with the ad SDK, and technical interaction events.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

You can manage, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android OS settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

B. Google Play In-App Billing

We provide optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs and the "Remove Ads" upgrade) through the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • All financial transactions, credit card handling, and payment processing are managed entirely by Google LLC as the merchant of record.
  • The App never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account information, or billing address.
  • The App receives only a purchase token and product identifier from Google Play to verify that a purchase succeeded and to grant your digital items.

4. Data Deletion & User Controls

Because Word Search Wonders does not operate a user account system or remote database:

  • Deleting Local Game Data: You can permanently erase all local game data, unlocked levels, coins, and preferences at any time by going to Settings > Data Management > Delete / Reset Saved Game Data in the App.
  • Uninstalling the App: Removing the App from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data files.
  • Third-Party Advertising Data: To delete or opt out of advertising identifiers collected by Google AdMob, use your device's built-in controls at Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Word Search Wonders is designed for a general audience of puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently collected through third-party services, please contact us immediately.
